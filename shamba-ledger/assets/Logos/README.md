# Logos

- `shamba-mark.svg`: the ShambaOS mark, a white leaf drawn as one round-capped stroke on a `shamba` (#355442) disc. The master artwork; every app icon is rendered from it. Its inks are fixed (white leaf, #355442 disc); it does not follow the theme.
- `shamba-app-icon.png`: the round launcher icon at 1024px, rendered from the mark.

Keep a quarter of the diameter clear on every side, show it at 32px or larger, and never recolour the disc or add effects.
